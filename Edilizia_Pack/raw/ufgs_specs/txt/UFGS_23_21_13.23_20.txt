2.1.9.1   
Welded Joints
Joints between sections of pipe and between pipe and fittings must be 
welded.  Joints between pipe and valves must be welded or flanged.  The 
welding must conform to requirements of paragraph entitled "Responsibility 
of Contractor for Fusion Welding."  Branch connections may be made with

acceptable without size limitations.  Branch outlet fittings where used 
must be forged, flared for improved flow where attached to the run, 
reinforced against external strains, and designed to withstand full 
pipe-bursting strength.

must be welded or flanged, faced true, provided with gaskets, and made 
perfectly square and tight.  Flanges must be forged steel, raised face, 
weld-neck type.  Slip-on flanges will not be allowed.  Gaskets for [HTW] 
[MTW] systems must be [metallic non-asbestos] [_____].

Each welder must be examined at the jobsite by the Contractor in the

Examination Results
The Contracting Officer must be provided with a listing of names and 
corresponding code markings.  Where a welder fails to meet the prescribed 
welding qualifications, that welder must be retested, and if he fails the 
second test, he must be disqualified for work on the project.

ASME B31.1
.
3.3.4   
Weld Inspection
Welds must be inspected for defects in accordance with the following:
a.  Cracks must not be acceptable regardless of length or location;
b.  Undercut must not be deeper than 5 percent of the base-metal thickness 
or
 0.79 mm
 1/32 inch
, whichever is less;
c.  Overlap must not be permitted.  The Contracting Officer reserves the 
right to further examine the welds by other means to establish the 
soundness of any weld.  Weld defects must be removed and repairs made 
to the weld, or the weld joints must be entirely removed and repairs 
made to the weld at no additional cost to the Government.  Repairing 
defective welds by adding weld material over the defect or by peening 
will not be permitted.  Welders responsible for defective welds may be 
required to requalify under paragraph entitled "Qualifications of 
Welders."
