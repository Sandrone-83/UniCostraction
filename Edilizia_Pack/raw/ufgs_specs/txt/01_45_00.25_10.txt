1.3   CMP SOFTWARE
CMP is an IL-4 web-based application capable of handling Controlled 
Unclassified Information (CUI) and other sensitive government data.  Do not 
enter, upload, transmit, or store information in CMP that exceeds the 
system authorization or applicable contract security requirements.
To ensure secure access, obtain and maintain credentials required for CMP 
access.  Authenticate using EAMS-A and EAMS-A MobileConnect.
CMP is authorized for deployment on mobile devices and is available on both 
iOS and Android platforms.  Contractors are authorized to use the CMP 
mobile application for the entry, management, and maintenance of contract 
data required by the contract using the approved authentication method.  
Subject to project-specific authorization and guidance, contractors may 
download CMP from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and use it 
securely in accordance with the approved authentication method and 
applicable security requirements.  Most applications are available on the 
CMP mobile application which includes offline capabilities when an internet 
connection is unavailable and automatic sync once reconnected.
CMP and the CMP Mobile Application minimum system requirements are 
available at the Kahua System Requirements website:
Kahua System Requirements
https://kahua.mcoutput.com/preview/Content/2-Using-Kahua/System-Settings/system-requireme
Contractors must begin populating required contract data within 7 calendar 
days of receipt of the Notice to Proceed or within 7 calendar days of 
receiving access to the contract in CMP.  CMP user guidance, access 
instructions, updates, and training information will be provided by the 
Government.  The common database will be updated each time a user saves an 
entry or change.
Notify the Contracting Officer of departing personnel immediately, not to 
exceed 72 hours after their departure for removal from CMP.
Log in to CMP at least every 35 days to maintain active user account.

1.5   CONTRACT ADMINISTRATION
The Government will use CMP to manage and administer this contract.  The 
Government and the Contractor will have different access authorities (roles 
or permissions) to the same contract database within CMP.  The joint 
Government-Contractor use of CMP facilitates electronic exchange of 
information and overall management of the contract.  Unless otherwise 
noted, use CMP to provide all data submissions required within this 
contract's specification, to include template forms and letters when 
available.
The Contractor accesses CMP to record, maintain, input, track, and 
electronically share information with the Government throughout the 
contract period via the following applications and application suites:
a. Administration
b. Document Management
c. Field Management
d. Cost Management
1.5.1   Correspondence And Electronic Communications
Manage contract correspondence and electronic communications as follows:
Exchange correspondence and other documents in electronic format via CMP to 
the maximum extent feasible.  Digitally sign all records requiring 
signatures in CMP.
Create, transmit, and track all official contract correspondence within CMP 
such that the system reflects current correspondence status (including 
attachments and related records).
When the Contracting Officer requires paper documents with original 
signatures, provide the paper originals as directed and upload a PDF copy 
to CMP.  Where an electronic copy is also used, the paper document governs 
in the event of discrepancy.

1.6   CMP CONTRACT DATABASE
The Government and the Contractor will use the same contract database 
within CMP with appropriate permissions.
The Government will enter the basic contract award data in CMP prior to 
granting the Contractor access.  Government entries applicable to the 
contractor in CMP will generally be related to submittal reviews, 
correspondence status, and Quality Assurance (QA), as well as other 
miscellaneous administrative information.
Contractor entries in CMP must establish, maintain, and update data 
throughout the duration of the contract.  Contractor entries generally 
include, but are not limited to, prime and subcontractor information, daily 
project data and associated information, quality control activities, 
submittals, requests for information (RFI), schedule updates, and payment 
requests.
1.6.1   Administration
The Contractor responsibilities for entries in CMP include but are not 
limited to the following items:
1.6.1.1   Contractor Information
Contractor's name, address, telephone number(s), management staff, and all 
required data fields in the Company and Contacts, Project, Project 
Directory, and other applications within CMP.
1.6.1.2   Subcontractor Information
Enter all subcontractor administrative data and information into CMP within 
7 calendar days of receiving access to the contract in CMP or within 7 
calendar days of signing the subcontract agreement.  This includes name, 
trade, address, phone number(s), and other required information for all 
subcontractors.  List subcontractors separately for each individual trade 
to be performed.
For each subcontractor, enter name, trade, address, phone number(s), and 
other required information within seven calendar days of signing the 
subcontract agreement.  List subcontractors separately for each individual 
trade to be performed.
1.6.1.3   Correspondence
Identify all Contractor correspondence to the Government with a serial 
number.  Prefix correspondence initiated by the Contractor's office with 
"S".  Number letters starting from 0001 (e.g., S-0001).  The Government's 
letters to the Contractor are prefixed with "C".  Request for Proposals are 
SECTION 01 45 00.25 10  Page 4

generated in CMP and prefixed with "RFP".
1.6.1.4   Equipment
Enter and maintain a current list of equipment planned for use or being 
used on the project site, including the most recent and planned equipment 
inspection dates in accordance with Section 01 45 00 QUALITY CONTROL.
1.6.1.5   Data, Reports and Templates
Track the status of the project using analytics, dashboards, and reports 
available in CMP.  These data tracking tools include but are not limited to 
weekly progress analytics, daily reports, Deficiency Tracking Log, 
Submittal Register Status, and Schedule and Progress Payment reporting.  
Where data can be entered in CMP, use the applicable output templates 
available in CMP for activities such as meetings, submittals, safety 
submissions and reporting, progress payments, preconstruction documents, 
and similar project deliverables.
When other specifications in this contract require the use of an ENG Form 
which is integrated in CMP as a template, enter the data directly into CMP 
instead of filling out the ENG Form and submitting as a PDF.
1.6.2   Document Management
Use the CMP Document Management applications suite to perform contract 
management functions specifically related to the management and 
distribution of information including drawing and specifications, RFIs, 
Submittals, correspondence, and other contract documents.
1.6.2.1   BIM
When other sections of this contract require Advanced Modeling, upload all 
BIM and other model files to the File Manager application in CMP.  Use the 
BIM application to view, compare, and annotate advanced model files.
When the contract requires Government collaboration, upload all advanced 
modeling files to CMP.  Pin all related CMP records to the current BIM 
files.
1.6.2.2   Correspondence
This application is the central log for all contract-related 
communications.  All official contract correspondence must occur in CMP.
1.6.2.3   Design Review
This application streamlines and enhances document reviews, including 
drawings, models, specifications and reports, and utilizes markups, 
annotations, and review and approval workflow to track progress and 
backcheck comments.
Initiate, manage and conduct all design reviews in CMP.
1.6.2.4   File Manager
Store project documentation not contained elsewhere in the system in an 
organized format.  File manager includes version history, viewing, markup, 
and external sharing capabilities.

Upload and store all contract photos and videos in the Media Manager 
application.
1.6.2.6   Meetings
Use this application to set up meetings, create agendas, record meeting 
minutes, track attendance, and assign and track items to be completed.
Schedule and document all meetings held with the Government in the CMP

1.6.2.7   Submittals
Use the "Submittal Register/Items" and Submittal Packages applications to 
create, track, manage, and markup submittals.  Manage all project 
submittals in CMP in accordance with Section 01 33 00 SUBMITTAL PROCEDURES.
Use the "Submittal Register/Items" application to update, maintain and 
track the contract submittal items.  The Government will provide the 
initial project submittal register.  Update and maintain the submittal 
register  daily in CMP.
Use the Submittal Packages application to package and transmit submittals 
to the Government for review and manage the submittal workflow.  Where 
referenced elsewhere in this contract, the terms submittal transmittals and 
ENG Form 4025 are synonymous with Submittal Packages in CMP.  Group 
submittal items from the same specification into submittal packages for 
Government review.  When samples, spare parts, mock-ups, color boards or 
hard copies are specifically required, provide documentation in the 
submittal package indicating that the physical submittal was transmitted.  
Do not group submittal items with variations in the same submittal package 
as submittal items without a variation request.
Contractors may request submittal items be added to CMP throughout the 
lifecycle of the project.  Approval and disapproval of Contractor requests 
are at the discretion of the Contracting Officer. 
1.6.2.8   Requests for Information (RFIs)
Create, submit, and track all RFIs for Government review and response in 
the RFI application.  RFIs must include a description with specific 
contract references, the information requested, a contractor 
recommendation, potential cost and or schedule impacts, linked schedule 
activity (if applicable), and any relevant reference files.  Review the 
status of RFIs in the weekly progress meeting in accordance with Section 
01 32 01.00 10 PROJECT SCHEDULE.

Use the CMP Field Management Application suite to manage safety, quality,

1.6.3.1.1   Accident Prevention Plan (APP)
Use the application to identify potential hazards and outline the measures 
that will be taken to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working 
environment in accordance with the EM 385-1-1.  Create, submit and maintain 
the Accident Prevention Plan (APP) in CMP.

1.6.3.2   Closeout
Use this application to track all tasks and activities required to closeout 
the project.
Use the CMP Closeout application to manage project red zone and closeout 
activities including all required contract deliverables, to include but not 
limited to, O&M manuals, as-built drawings, spare parts, administrative 
closeout, transfers of construction, warranty, and remediation of all 
deficiencies.
Maintain and link all applicable records to the redline drawings in CMP as 
required in Section 01 45 00 QUALITY CONTROL and Section 01 78 00 CLOSEOUT 
SUBMITTALS.
1.6.3.3   Commissioning
Use this application to manage the commissioning process including 
equipment lists, pre-functional checklists, and functional performance 
tests.
Manage all commissioning documentation in CMP.  Develop commissioning 
documentation (checklists, test reports, etc.) directly in CMP or upload as 
complete PDFs in the Commissioning application for the record and 
Government review.  Create or provide all required commissioning 
documentation in accordance with the applicable contract specification 
sections.
1.6.3.4   Deficiencies
Use this application to create, track, and resolve quality, safety and 
environmental deficiencies, document punch list inspections, and manage the 
process through correction and verification.  Document all deficiencies as 
applicable in Sections 01 45 00 QUALITY CONTROL, 01 35 26 GOVERNMENTAL 
SAFETY REQUIREMENTS, 01 57 19 TEMPORARY ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROLS and the 
EM 385-1-1.
Identify, enter, track, and resolve deficiencies in CMP, including those 
entered by the government, and manage resolution to completion.  Pin the 
location of all deficiencies and punch list items to drawing within CMP.  
Link each deficiency to the applicable schedule activity.  Payment will not 
be made for activities with unresolved deficiencies.  Capture and upload 
photographs of the deficiencies and obtain the government concurrence or 
verification of the remediation.  Use the Deficiencies application to 
document all punch-out inspections and pre-final or final inspections, in 
accordance with specification Section 01 45 00 QUALITY CONTROL.

1.6.3.5   Definable Features of Work (DFOW)
Use this application to create, submit, and finalize the contract DFOWs and 
link the appropriate DFOW to other records within CMP.
Link all project records, including but not limited to RFIs, Submittals, 
work performed, progress payment items, schedule activities and daily 
reports, to the associated DFOW.
1.6.3.6   Field Observations
Use this application to capture all actionable quality, safety, and 
environmental field observations.  Pin the location on the drawings, assign 
responsibility, and track to resolution all Field Observations, including 
those documented by the Government.
1.6.3.7   Mishap Reporting
Provide all information and documentation pertaining to accident, near miss 
and injury reporting in the Mishap Reporting application.  Enter all 
required ENG Form 3394 data in the system for Near Miss, Initial Accident 
Reports, and Final Accident reports, and attach all reference documentation 
pertinent to the mishap investigation as required by the Contracting 
Officer.  Follow all reporting requirements for accidents and incidents as 
required in EM 385-1-1, Section 01 35 26 GOVERNMENTAL SAFETY REQUIREMENTS, 
and as required by any other applicable federal, state, or local agencies.
1.6.3.8   Inspections
Use this application to catalog all required project inspections and 
testing.  Assign a responsible individual to track each inspection to 
completion.
All project inspections, including but not limited to, safety inspections, 
material inspections, inspections of completed work, QC and third-party 
testing must be documented and managed in this application.  Include 
documentation of any required corrective actions.
1.6.3.9   Daily Reports
Use this application to record and document all daily activities on the 
project, including weather, labor, equipment, and materials.
Enter Daily Reports in CMP and submit for review in accordance with Section 
01 45 00 QUALITY CONTROL and Section 01 35 26 GOVERNMENTAL SAFETY 
REQURIEMENTS.
Update data and documentation in CMP daily, including but not limited to, 
QC documentation, RFIs, submittals, and modifications.  CMP includes an 
analytics functionality to consolidate the necessary data from all 
applications into the Daily Summary Report.
1.6.3.10   Three-Phase Quality Control  
Use this application to manage the Preparatory, Initial, and Follow-Up 
phases for all Definable Features of Work.
Use the Three-Phase Quality Control application to track all activities and 
documentation for each DFOW including preparatory phase meeting, initial

the preparatory phase, all inspection documentation, test reports, and 
location for all initial and follow-up inspections, and link applicable

1.6.4   Cost Management 
Use the CMP Cost Management application suite to manage contract financial 
interaction with the Government.
1.6.4.1   Contract Transfer - DD Form 1354
This application provides the ability to leverage facility and contract 
data to create a DD Form 1354.  Use the DD Form 1354 application to create 
the draft, interim, and final versions of DD Form 1354 and manage the 
review workflow
1.6.4.2   Issues And Potential Changes
Use this application to log and track to resolution of all issues that have 
potential impacts to the project.  Records generated in their respective 
CMP applications (such as RFIs or deficiencies) may be processed into an 
Issue when escalation is required for resolution.
Issues that result in a contract change will be processed into a potential 
change, and documentation tracked within this application.  Provide 
proposal information in response to requests for proposal within the 
application in accordance with all applicable contract and FAR clause 
requirements.  This includes but is not limited to cost and time impact 
information as data and an attached PDF containing required backup 
documentation.
1.6.4.3   Progress Payments
Use this application to create, submit, review and track progress and final 
payments.  Update the work completed under the Contract at least monthly, 
measured as percent or as specific quantities.  Input the draft Schedule of 
Values (SOV) amounts into the application and submit the pencil copy of the 
cost-loaded SOV for Government review and concurrence based on actual 
progress and requested pay amounts per activity.  Utilize the application 
to track total amounts invoiced to date for each line item, withholdings, 
stored materials, and executed contract changes.
Incorporate the Government-approved draft SOV into the formal periodic 
schedule update submission in accordance with Section 01 32 01.00 10 
PROJECT SCHEDULE.  Upon completion of the Government periodic schedule 
review, CMP will integrate approved cost-loaded SOV data into the Progress 
Payment application.  Use this imported data to create the formal progress 
payment request and generate the prompt payment certification directly 
within the application.  Complete the process by submitting the signed 
prompt payment certification, the formal payment request, and all 
supporting reference documentation through the application.
