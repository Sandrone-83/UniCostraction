3.2.3.1   
Fuel Piping Pressure Tests
[Pneumatically test gas piping at 1 1/2 times operating pressure and check

3.2.3.2   
Fire Tests for Nonelectrical Heating Equipment
Test combustion controls and equipment with specified fuel at 100 percent 
full rated load.  During tests, verify proper operation of controls. 
Adjust burners for maximum efficiency using Orsat or similar apparatus. 
Maintain firing for at least four hours [, and where high-low-off 
combustion controls are provided, operate the heating equipment for one 
hour at low fire and 3 hours at high fire].  For acceptable combustion 
efficiency, allow maximum 4.5 percent carbon dioxide in flue gases.
3.2.3.3   
Insulation-Resistance Tests for Electrical Equipment
At the completion of wiring, test 600 volt wiring to verify that no short 
circuits exist before or after the attachment of electrical heating 
equipment to the power source.  Make tests with an instrument which 
applies a voltage of approximately 500 volts for a direct reading of 
insulation resistance.
3.2.3.4   
Operational Tests
After completing fire tests and insulation-resistance tests, operate 
equipment continuously under varying load conditions to verify functioning 
of combustion controls, electrical controls, flame safeguard controls, 
safety interlocks, and specified operating sequence.  Run each test for a 
minimum period of one hour.
